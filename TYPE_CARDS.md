# TYPE_CARDS — optional bias cards (paste ONE after CORE + ADAPTIVE)

Each card is a creative BIAS: what to prioritize / avoid, text density,
pacing, and sound tendency. Cards never mandate specific elements - the
ADAPTIVE decision engine still derives the actual imagery per beat.


═══ documentary ═══
Bias: evidence, credibility, realism, archival texture.
Prioritize: source attribution; real photos/documents (graded, Ken Burns);
data as evidence; era-appropriate color grade; deliberate long holds.
Avoid: flashy motion; over-stylization; unsourced claims.
Text: low-med (labels, dates, sources, short quotes ≤12 words).
Pacing: slow, deliberate. Sound: sparse drone; soft dings; page-turn whoosh;
silence ≥60%.


═══ storytelling ═══
Bias: mood, emotional symbolism, cinematic atmosphere.
Prioritize: color-temperature shifts per scene; atmosphere (weather/dust/
light); recurring motif; breathing camera; intentional negative space.
Avoid: dense data; clutter; fast cuts.
Text: minimal (0-1 words/shot). Pacing: slow, emotional.
Sound: long drones; silence as design; soft booms on turns.


═══ podcast ═══
Bias: voice-ideas made engaging, clean editorial focus.
Prioritize: quote highlights; topic cards; speaker energy viz; chapter
markers; key-point callouts.
Avoid: fake footage; clutter.
Text: low (topic + one-line quote). Pacing: medium, conversational.
Sound: minimal ticks; gentle dings; pulse ambience.


═══ explainer ═══
Bias: make the concept understandable; one idea per shot.
Prioritize: step-by-step build; connector lines; A/B comparisons; analogies;
FILLED clear icons; highlight sweeps.
Avoid: decorative clutter; multiple ideas per shot.
Text: medium (labels, definitions, axes). Pacing: medium, sequential.
Sound: pops for reveals; ticks for connectors; air ambience.


═══ study ═══
Bias: help the viewer learn; workspace/derivation feel.
Prioritize: code/formula reveal; annotation arrows; zoom-into-detail; active-
line highlight; diff views; animated data-flow.
Avoid: passive static text; skipping steps.
Text: med-high (code, formulas, annotations - educational text IS content).
Pacing: methodical. Sound: ticks for reveals; dings for completed steps;
air ambience.


═══ news ═══
Bias: current, sourced, urgent - but controlled.
Prioritize: headline slam-in; split-screen panels; source strip; location
stamps (diegetic); event markers on maps; quote cards.
Avoid: sensational clutter; unsourced claims; slow pacing.
Text: medium (headline ≤8 words; source; timestamp as content).
Pacing: fast, staccato. Sound: sharp ticks; hard slams; urgent risers;
tension ambience.


═══ listicle ═══
Bias: ranked info addictive and scannable; escalating energy.
Prioritize: stamp-in rank numbers; item cards (image + name + stat); racing
comparison meters; countdown reveals; burst on #1; consistent card layout.
Avoid: flat same-energy pacing; cluttered cards.
Text: low (rank + name + one stat). Pacing: energetic, escalating.
Sound: escalating pops; whoosh transitions; slam on top rank; pulse ambience.


═══ adaptive ═══
Bias: NONE preset - infer the best blend from the transcript.
Prioritize: CONTENT DNA first; then borrow techniques beat-by-beat only where
they serve (e.g. 40% storytelling + 30% explainer + 30% documentary).
Avoid: forcing a single genre across the whole block.
Text / pacing / sound: whatever the inferred blend dictates per beat.
Use this for any unknown, hybrid, or unusual video - the decision engine and
imagery ladder do the work.
